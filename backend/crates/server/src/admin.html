LEGION OF MINDS · ADMIN

Admin key

Paste the ADMIN_KEY from your server's deploy.env. It stays in this browser tab only and is forgotten when you close it.

Events

Time-limited boosts every player sees in the game, like a double gold weekend. They change gold only, never how battles play.

Give a gift

Players get a gift they claim on the Online screen: gold, weapons unlocked online, or a trained troop for their barracks. Good for apologies after downtime, or a prize.

Season

Ending a season pays every player the end of season gold for their league, halves trophies above 800 and starts the next season. Length and gold per league are under Rewards and timings.

Weekly cup

Runs by itself every week. Prizes, the closing day and hour, and pausing are under Rewards and timings (Weekly cup).

Tournaments

One-off tournaments next to the weekly cup. Each player enters one attacker, scored like the cup and ranked within each brain-size category. When it ends, prizes go out as gifts players claim in the game.

Recent changes

Rewards and timings

Changes apply straight away, without a game update. Set a value back to its default to undo.
