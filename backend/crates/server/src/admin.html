LEGION OF MINDS · ADMIN

Admin key

Paste the ADMIN_KEY from your server's deploy.env. It stays in this browser tab only and is forgotten when you close it.

Events

Time-limited boosts every player sees in the game, like a double gold weekend. They change gold only, never how battles play.

Give gold

Players get a gift they claim on the Online screen. Good for apologies after downtime, or a prize.

Season

Ending a season pays every player the end of season gold for their league, halves trophies above 800 and starts the next season. Length and gold per league are under Rewards and timings.

Recent changes

Rewards and timings

Changes apply straight away, without a game update. Set a value back to its default to undo.
