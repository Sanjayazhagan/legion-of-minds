# Become a God: trailer script

A 90-second announcement trailer for the game (repo name Legion of Minds; the title screen says BECOME A GOD). The shot-by-shot picture plan is in [STORYBOARD.md](STORYBOARD.md).

**Logline:** Every soldier in this game starts out knowing nothing. You give it a body, a plan and a lesson, and it learns to fight on your own computer. Then you find out what you made.

**Tone:** Dry and a little wry at the start, then building to an epic finish. The narrator is a calm, older voice, like a teacher who has seen a thousand recruits. The joke underneath: the "god" is a player at a desk, and the miracle is a learning curve.

**Running time:** 1:30 · **Aspect:** 16:9, captured at 1280x800 and letterboxed to 1920x1080 · **VO:** about 170 words

## ACT I: THE CLAY (0:00 to 0:16)

**FADE IN.**

**EXT. TRAINING ARENA, A GREEN CHECKERED FIELD. DAY.**

A single troop stands near a red tower. It is untrained. It turns left. It turns right. It walks straight into the tower's range and stops to look at nothing.

> **NARRATOR (V.O.)**
> This is a soldier on its first day.

It swings its axe at the empty air. An arrow hits it.

> **NARRATOR (V.O.)**
> It doesn't know what an axe is for. It doesn't know what a tower is.

It falls over. A small counter in the corner reads **Drill #1**.

> **NARRATOR (V.O.)**
> Nobody is going to tell it.

**SMASH CUT TO BLACK.**

SUPER, white on black: **NOBODY PROGRAMMED THESE SOLDIERS.**

Beat. Second line fades in under it: **THEY LEARNED.**

## ACT II: THE BREATH OF LIFE (0:16 to 0:34)

**INT. RECRUIT SCREEN.**

A cursor types percentages into class rows. Mage 50. Healer 30. Fighter 20. The total ticks to **100%** and turns green.

> **NARRATOR (V.O.)**
> So you start where every god starts. With the clay.

The preview card at the bottom resolves its name: **the Holy Sorcerer of Iron Arts**. A wizard hat, a staff, an axe on its back.

> **NARRATOR (V.O.)**
> A body.

The cursor moves to the Training Plan column. Heal allies goes up to 40%. Stay alive 20%.

> **NARRATOR (V.O.)**
> And a reason to live.

Click: **Recruit (50g) and train.**

## ACT III: THE LESSON (0:34 to 0:56)

**INT. TRAINING SCREEN.**

The live drill plays on the left. On the right the step counter climbs and the chart crawls along the bottom: wins at zero.

> **NARRATOR (V.O.)**
> Then you let it practise. Thousands of times a second, on your own machine.

Close on the readout: **3342 steps/s on your CPU**.

The chart sits flat. The troop in the drill keeps away from the tower and waits out the clock.

> **NARRATOR (V.O.)**
> For a while it learns the wrong thing. That hiding is safer than winning.

Hold on the flat line. Then it jumps: the green "wins" line shoots up.

> **NARRATOR (V.O.)**
> And then, one day...

The skill badges light up one after another, each with a soft chime: **Fights guards. Breaks buildings. Stays alive. Wins drills.**

> **NARRATOR (V.O.)**
> ...it gets it.

## ACT IV: THE WAR (0:56 to 1:20)

**EXT. THE FORT. BATTLE.**

Five names on the right: Akira, Yuki, Sora, Kaito, Daichi. The cursor drops them one by one outside the red zone. Click **FIGHT!**.

Music kicks in hard. The squad pours in. The healer stays behind the fighters. The fighters take the guards first, then turn on the tower.

> **NARRATOR (V.O.)**
> You don't control them in battle. You already did your part.

The destruction counter climbs: 22%... 50%. The first star fills. The red castle collapses into rubble. Second star.

**EXT. HOME KEEP. DEFENCE.**

Blue towers. A raiding party of skeletons comes over the edge. Four defenders step out of tower cover to meet them, then fall back inside it.

> **NARRATOR (V.O.)**
> Your defenders learned too. The same way.

SUPER, small, top right: **0% of your base lost.**

**INT. TOURNAMENT: THE PROVING GROUNDS.**

The ranking table. The top row glows gold: **Sora, 271**. At the bottom of the screen: **Champion: Sora**.

> **NARRATOR (V.O.)**
> Same mix, same plan, two different soldiers. No two of yours will fight the same way.

## ACT V: THE PANTHEON (1:20 to 1:30)

**INT. GALLERY.**

Slow push across rows of characters: Fighter, Archer, Healer, Mage, then the blends: Battle Priest, Holy Sorcerer, Arcane Crusader of Swift Arts, Hexed Guardian of Wild Arts. At the end of the last row, a lone skeleton Raider.

> **NARRATOR (V.O.)**
> You didn't make soldiers.

Beat.

> **NARRATOR (V.O.)**
> You made minds.

**SMASH CUT TO TITLE.**

TITLE CARD, gold on near-black, the game's own pixel font: **BECOME A GOD**

Under it, three words appear one at a time on the music hits: **TRAIN THEM. TRUST THEM. RULE.**

Small text at the bottom: **Every troop learns on your own computer.**

**FADE OUT.**

## Truth check

Every claim in the VO matches what the game does today, so the trailer doesn't promise anything that isn't there:

- "Nobody programmed these soldiers / they learned": troops are PPO agents trained from random on the player's CPU (`crates/godcore/src/ppo.rs`).
- "Thousands of times a second, on your own machine": about 3,000 to 10,000 steps/s on a 4-core CPU (game/README.md), and the training screen prints the live rate.
- "Hiding is safer than winning": the real plateau found in the prototype ("hide until timeout", prototype/README.md). The game now punishes running out of time, but the plateau-then-jump shape is still how learning looks on the chart.
- "You don't control them in battle": you only place troops; the brains act.
- "Same mix, same plan, two different soldiers": the README results show different seeds of the same troop disagreeing (docs/HOW_THE_RL_WORKS.md, section 3).
- Online wars are **not** in the trailer, because they aren't finished yet.
