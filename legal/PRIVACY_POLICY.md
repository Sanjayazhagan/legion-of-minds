# Privacy Policy

Version 2026-11-24. DRAFT: not yet reviewed by a lawyer.

This policy explains what Legion of Minds collects, why, who it is shared with, how long it is kept, and how to delete it. "We" means [OPERATOR NAME], [POSTAL ADDRESS], contact [CONTACT EMAIL].

## The short version

- Offline play sends us nothing unless you choose to share play data. Your troops, bases, training and clips stay on your computer.
- Online play needs a Google or Discord sign-in. We get an account number and a name from them, not your email address or password.
- To make online battles fair, the server keeps your published base, the armies you send and the troops' trained brains, and other players see your base and name.
- Sharing play data to help improve the game is optional and off unless you say yes. It is tied to a random number, not to you, it is deleted after 180 days, and you can delete it yourself in Settings.
- You can delete your online account in Settings, and it is removed from the server straight away.

## 1. Playing offline

The game saves your troops, base, settings, training progress and the result of the PC speed test in a folder on your computer (Settings shows where). Battle clips you save go to your Videos folder. None of this is sent to us (play data, section 6, is separate and only if you say yes). The PC speed test only measures your computer and its graphics card locally.

## 2. Signing in

When you sign in with Google we ask only for "openid profile"; with Discord only for "identify". From that we keep:

- which service you used and the account number it gives us (so you get the same game account next time);
- a display name taken from your first name or username. You can change it in the game.

We don't receive your email address, password, friends list or avatar. When you sign in, you see Google's or Discord's own page, and their privacy policies cover what they do.

We keep a sign-in session for up to 30 days. Only a scrambled (hashed) copy of its key is stored on the server.

## 3. What the server keeps while you play online

- Your game account: display name, gold, trophies, league, town hall level, weapons and troop levels bought online, and season results.
- Your published defences: base layout and defending troops, including their trained brains, plus the game version they were made with.
- Raids: who attacked whom, the army used (troops and brains), where they were placed, the random seed and the result, so the server can re-run the battle and you can watch replays.
- Weekly cup entries and clan war bases and attacks.
- Clans: membership and role, clan name and description, chat messages, and troops you lend to the clan.
- Your internet (IP) address is used briefly to limit how many requests each address can make, to stop flooding. The game server holds this in memory only and doesn't write it to its database. The web server in front of it may keep standard access logs, including IP addresses, for up to [14] days for security.

## 4. Why we use it (legal bases)

- To run the online game you asked for: accounts, battles, leaderboards, cup, clans (performing our agreement with you).
- To keep the game fair and secure: re-running battles, catching tampered results, rate limits, enforcing the Terms (our legitimate interest in a fair and working game).
- Optional play data and ratings (section 6): only with your consent, which you can take back at any time.

We don't sell your data, show ads, or use your data to build an advertising profile.

## 5. Who can see what

- Other players see your display name, trophies, league, clan, your published base and its troops (to raid it and watch replays), your cup results, and what you write in clan chat (clan members only).
- To run a raid, the attacker's game downloads your published defence, including the troops' brains. A determined player could copy them.
- Service providers that process data for us: [Microsoft Azure] hosts the server and database in [REGION]; Google and Discord handle sign-in; if play data sharing is on, those events go to our own game server (no outside analytics company receives them). If you got the game on Steam, Valve handles the purchase under its own privacy policy.
- We may disclose data if the law requires it, or to protect players and the service from fraud or abuse.

## 6. Optional play data and ratings (off by default)

The game can share play data with us to help improve it. It is off unless you say yes.

- **Asking you.** The game asks once, on first launch (or on the first launch after the update that added it). You can turn it on or off at any time in Settings, where "What is sent" lists every kind of event the game can send. Our server refuses anything not on that list.
- **Not tied to you.** Events carry a random install number made on your computer when you said yes, and a random number for each time the game runs. They don't include your account, sign-in, display name, email address, troop names, troops' trained brains, saved games, clan chat or any file from your computer. The game server doesn't store your IP address with them (the web server logs in section 3 still apply). Because the install number links your events together, the law may still treat them as personal data, so we handle them under this policy.
- **What is sent.** How play goes: the game opening and closing (days since your first game, operating system, processor cores, graphics chip, what training runs on, the biggest brain your PC can train, minutes played and frame rate); which screens you open and how far you get through the new player tour; battle results (type of battle, stars, win or loss, your squad's classes, levels and brain sizes); training runs (class, level, brain size, how long they took, drill win rate); the PC speed test result; sign-in working or failing (the service and error code, never the account); online errors; crashes (the error message, with your folder names removed, and where in the game's code it happened); and the game and battle rules version.
- **Ratings.** If sharing is on, the game may ask you to rate your first raid and your first training from 1 to 5. A rating is sent like any other event. You can skip it.
- **Before you answer.** Until you answer, the game holds events from that session in memory. If you say yes, they are sent; if you say no, they are thrown away. Events that can't be sent yet wait on your computer in a file named play-data.json.
- **How long.** The server deletes play data after 180 days. We may keep totals that can't identify anyone (for example, "how many players finished the tour") after that.
- **Deleting it.** Settings, "What is sent", "Delete my play data" deletes everything sent from your computer from the server straight away and turns sharing off. Turning it on again starts a new install number. Deleting your online account doesn't touch play data, because the two aren't linked.
- **Why.** Only with your consent, to find where new players get stuck, balance classes, and fix crashes and slow frames. We don't sell it, use it for ads, or combine it with your online account.
- If you are under 18, please leave it off unless a parent or guardian agrees.

You can also give feedback directly, for example in our Discord server, where Discord's own privacy policy applies.

## 7. How long we keep it

- Raids and their replays: about 14 days, then deleted, along with old published bases no raid still needs.
- Sign-in sessions: until you sign out or after 30 days.
- Play data, if you share it: 180 days, or until you delete it in Settings.
- Weekly cup entries: about 4 weeks after the cup ends.
- Your account and its current base: until you delete the account, or we close it after a long time without play.
- Server backups: overwritten within [30] days, so deleted data leaves the backups by then.

## 8. Deleting your account and your other rights

In the game: Settings, Account, "Delete online account". This removes from the server, straight away and for good: your account and sign-in links, sessions, published bases, raids you were in (for both sides), cup and season entries, clan membership, lent troops and everything you wrote in clan chat. If you led a clan, the next senior member takes over. Your offline save on your computer is not touched: delete the save folder shown in Settings to remove that too. Play data is deleted separately (section 6).

You can also write to [CONTACT EMAIL] to ask for a copy of your data, to correct it, to delete it, or to object to how we use it. We'll answer within 30 days. Depending on where you live (for example under the EU and UK GDPR, or India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act), you may also complain to your data protection authority.

## 9. Children

Online play is for players aged 13 and over, and players under 18 need a parent's or guardian's agreement. We don't knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has an online account, write to [CONTACT EMAIL] and we will delete it.

## 10. Security and where data is stored

Connections to the server are encrypted (HTTPS). Sign-in keys are stored hashed. The server is in [REGION]; if you live elsewhere, your data travels there, with the protections this policy describes. No system is perfectly secure, and we'll tell affected players and the authorities about a serious breach as the law requires.

## 11. Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, the game will show the new version before you play again. The version date is at the top.
