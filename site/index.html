Legion of Minds

Every troop is a tiny AI that learns on your own PC. You recruit it, train it, and send it to raid. Then you watch what it figured out.

Download for Windows How it plays

Free · Windows 10 and 11, 64-bit · No graphics card needed

You don't control your troops. You teach them.

There's no unit micro in battle. Each troop is a small neural network that learns by playing thousands of practice drills. How well it fights depends on how you trained it.

  1. Recruit

    Mix any classes by percentage, like Mage 50, Healer 30, Fighter 20, and set what the reward cares about.

  2. Train

    Give it a budget of practice steps. Watch the newest brain play live drills while the win rate climbs.

  3. Raid

    Place up to five troops outside a base and press Fight. Earn stars at 50%, at the Town Hall, and at 100%.

  4. Defend

    Train defenders, build towers and cannons, and publish your base. Other players raid it while you're away.

More training, smarter troops

Each level raises how many practice steps a troop can learn from. Levelling costs gold, and your Town Hall sets the highest level.

Eleven classes, endless blends

Pick one class or blend several. The blend sets the stats, the gear on the troop's back, and what its drills reward. The same blend trained two different ways plays like two different troops.

Inside the game

Seven weapons with their own skills, team training for squads that learn to call out targets, tournaments, and a home base to build.

Fair by design

Every raid is re-checked

Battles replay exactly the same on any computer. The server re-runs every online raid itself, so a faked win doesn't count.

Your PC does the learning

Training runs on your processor, or on your graphics card for big brains. Nothing is trained in the cloud.

Play offline any time

Campaign bases, raid waves and tournaments work without an account. Sign in with Google or Discord to raid other players.

Download

The installer puts the game in your user folder, so it doesn't ask for administrator rights. Your saves stay in %APPDATA%\legion-of-minds through updates.
