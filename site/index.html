How it plays

Four moves. One season.

There's no unit micro. Each troop is a neural network that learns from thousands of practice drills, so how it fights comes down to how you trained it.

01

Recruit

Blend classes by percentage, like Mage 50, Healer 30, Fighter 20, and pick what its training rewards.

02

Train

Give it a budget of practice steps and watch the newest brain play live drills as its win rate climbs.

03

Raid

Place up to five troops and press Fight. Stars come at 50%, at the Town Hall, and at 100%.

04

Defend

Train defenders, build towers and publish your base. Other players raid it while you're away.

Training camp

More reps. Smarter troops.

Each level raises how many practice steps a troop can learn from. Levelling costs gold, and your Town Hall sets the cap. Bigger brains, from 64 to 256 neurons a layer, learn more but train slower.

The roster

Eleven classes. Endless blends.

Pick one class or mix several. The blend sets the stats, the gear on its back and what its drills reward. Train the same blend two ways and you get two different troops.

Inside the game

Build the base. Run the squad.

Seven weapons with their own skills, squads that learn to call targets to each other, tournaments, and a home base to build.

Fair play

No faked wins. No cloud.

100%

Of online raids re-checked

Battles replay the same on every computer, so the server re-runs each online raid itself. A faked win doesn't count.

0

Cloud training

Your processor does the learning, or your graphics card for big brains. Your troops are yours.

4

Offline bases to beat

Outpost, Fort, Twin Gate and Citadel, plus raid waves and tournaments, all without an account. Sign in with Google or Discord to raid players.

Train them.Then let go.

The installer puts the game in your user folder, so it never asks for admin rights. Saves stay in %APPDATA%\legion-of-minds through updates.
